# Progression: from settlement to electricity

The town advances through five eras. **Milestones** (people, homes, winters
survived, goods produced, techs learned) open the next era. Within an era, your
people's **knowledge** is spent on **techs** in a branching tree: each era offers more
techs than you need, so the order and the path are yours. Techs unlock buildings,
upgrade existing ones, change rules (illness, tool wear, trade) and bring new goods.
When an era begins, the town rebuilds into that era's style.

Knowledge per month = 2 + 0.25 per adult, plus schools and libraries later.

## Eras

| Era | Feel | Opens with | Milestones to reach the next era |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Settlement | Wooden huts, foraging | Houses, gatherers, hunters, woodcutters, foresters, stockpiles, wells | 20 people, 5 houses, 2 winters survived, 3 techs |
| 2 Village | Timber framing, tiled roofs, farming | - | 40 people, 1500 crops harvested, a trading post, 5 Village techs |
| 3 Medieval town | Stone, markets, faith | - | 80 people, 10 stone houses, 1000 bread made, 6 Medieval techs |
| 4 Industrial | Brick, coal, steam mills | - | 150 people, 2000 coal mined, a hospital, 6 Industrial techs |
| 5 Electric | Power lines, streetlights, clinics | - | (final era) |

## Tech tree

Branches: **Food**, **Industry**, **Society**. Arrows are prerequisites.

### 1 Settlement
- Agriculture (Food) - crop fields.
- Fishing (Food) - fishing docks.
- Herb lore (Society) - herbalists.
- Stonework (Industry) - quarries, storage barns.
- Hide working (Industry) - tailors.

### 2 Village
- Crop rotation (Food, needs Agriculture) - fields yield 25% more.
- Animal husbandry (Food) - hunters' game recovers faster and they bring more hides.
- Smithing (Industry) - iron mines, blacksmiths.
- Better tools (Industry, needs Smithing) - tools last two and a half times as long.
- Masonry (Industry, needs Stonework) - stone houses and chapels; fires break out half as often.
- Medicine (Society, needs Herb lore) - illness 40% rarer, herbs cure more surely.
- Trade (Society) - trading posts; merchants are drawn to the town and visit more often.
- Town planning (Society) - town halls (nomads), roads 20% faster.

### 3 Medieval town (stage 2)
Milling and baking (wheat to flour to bread), orchards, sheep pastures and wool,
taverns, faith (chapels, happiness), schools (knowledge), markets that deliver to
homes, stone roads.

### 4 Industrial (stage 3)
Coal mining, brickworks and brick houses, steelworks, textile mills, hospitals with
doctors, libraries, horse carts that haul more.

### 5 Electric (stage 4)
Coal power station and power lines, electric workshops (faster production),
streetlights, clinics and vaccination (illness rare), modern schools.
