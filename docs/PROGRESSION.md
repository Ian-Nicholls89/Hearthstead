# Progression: from settlement to the stars

The town advances through eight eras. **Milestones** (people, homes, winters
survived, goods produced, techs learned) open the next era. Within an era, your
people's **knowledge** is spent on **techs** in a branching tree: each era offers more
techs than you need, so the order and the path are yours. Techs unlock buildings,
upgrade existing ones, change rules and bring new goods. When an era begins the town
rebuilds into that era's style. The game ends well or badly depending on how you got
there.

Knowledge per month = 2 + 0.25 per adult, plus schools, universities and libraries.

| # | Era | Brings | Look | Milestones to leave it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Settlement | foraging, huts | wooden huts | 20 people, 5 houses, 2 winters, 3 techs |
| 2 | Village | farming, first trade | timber cottages | 40 people, 1500 crops harvested, a trading post, 5 techs |
| 3 | Small town | stone, bread, wool, markets, faith, schools | stone houses | 70 people, 8 stone houses, 1000 bread, 6 techs |
| 4 | Large town | guilds, university, printing, banks, harbour | grand stone, squares | 120 people, a university, 3000 goods traded, 6 techs |
| 5 | Industrial revolution | coal, steam factories, rail, brick terraces | brick and chimneys | 200 people, 5000 coal, a railway station, 6 techs |
| 6 | City | electricity, trams, hospitals, apartments | city blocks, streetlights | 300 people, power to every home, a hospital, 6 techs |
| 7 | Modern | cars, oil and gas, computers, supermarkets, renewables | modern buildings | 400 people, 6 techs, climate below the danger line |
| 8 | Utopia | fusion, automation, vertical farms, zero waste, space programme | sleek future town | (ending) |

## Climate

From the Industrial revolution a **climate meter** shows. Coal, oil, gas and power
stations raise it; forests, renewables and green techs lower it. Dirty techs are the
quick wins; clean ones cost more knowledge. As it rises: hotter summers and harsher
winters, failed harvests, storms that damage buildings, and rising water that floods
the banks (the water level visibly climbs). It also gates the Modern-to-Utopia
milestone.

## Endings

- **Space expansion (good):** in the Utopia era, build the Starport - the last great
  project. When it is finished your people leave for the stars.
- **Harmony (good):** reach Utopia with a healthy planet and a happy town.
- **Climate collapse (bad):** the climate meter reaches its limit and the
  civilisation falls.
- **Lost (bad):** hunger, cold or illness leave the town empty (already in the game).

Every ending shows the civilisation's story: eras reached and when, the branches
favoured, peak population, born and lost, the planet's health, key discoveries -
written from the player's own choices.

## Tech tree

Branches: **Food**, **Industry**, **Society** (from the Industrial era, cleaner and
dirtier choices within each). Prerequisites are named.

### 1 Settlement (in the game)
Agriculture (fields), Fishing (docks), Stonework (quarries, barns), Hide working
(tailors), Herb lore (herbalists).

### 2 Village (in the game)
Crop rotation (+25% harvests), Animal husbandry (game recovers, more hides),
Smithing (mines, blacksmiths), Better tools (tools last 2.5x), Masonry (stone houses,
chapels, fewer fires), Medicine (illness 40% rarer), Trade (trading posts, merchants
twice as often), Town planning (town halls, faster roads).

### 3 Small town (in the game)
Milling (windmills: wheat to flour), Baking (bakeries: flour and firewood to bread,
needs Milling), Orchards (apples, picked late summer to autumn), Shepherding (pastures:
wool and mutton, needs Husbandry), Weaving (weavers: wool to coats, which keep out
more cold), Paving (roads 25% faster), Faith (chapels lift spirits 60% more, needs
Masonry), Schooling (schools: teachers add knowledge), Brewing (taverns: happier
homes), Markets (markets carry food and firewood to nearby homes, needs Trade).

### 4 Large town
Guilds (workshop bonuses), university (knowledge), printing (cheaper techs), banking
(better trade), harbour and ships, aqueducts (health), glassworks.

### 5 Industrial revolution
Coal mining, steam engines and factories, brickworks and brick terraces, steelworks,
railway, textile mills, sanitation and hospitals; reforestation and efficiency as the
cleaner paths.

### 6 City
Electricity and power stations (coal or hydro), trams, apartments, modern medicine,
schools for all, streetlights, waste collection.

### 7 Modern
Cars and roads, oil and gas, computers (knowledge), supermarkets, solar and wind,
recycling, public transport.

### 8 Utopia
Fusion power, automation (fewer workers needed), vertical farms, zero waste,
climate repair, the space programme and the Starport.
